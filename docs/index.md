## About Me

That experience led me to pursue an Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Cybersecurity with a Local Area Network Systems – Cybersecurity Specialization. I quickly found I had a knack for the concepts, and I'm now building hands-on skills through CTFs, labs, and networking studies.
